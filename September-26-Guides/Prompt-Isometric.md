CRAFT
- Same projection, light and materials in every scene.
- Motion has a purpose. Nothing spins just for decoration.
- One accent colour means [ONE MEANING, e.g. "error", "value lost to waiting"] everywhere and is never used for anything else.
- Palette: [MOOD, e.g. "blue, colourful but calm"]. Check it for colour-blind safety.
